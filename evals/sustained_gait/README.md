# Six-step experiment evidence

Read `docs/FREEBASE_SUSTAINED_PROBE.md` for scope, commands and exact narrow acceptance. This is one continuous finite episode on the unchanged original licensed model, not walking/hardware qualification.

- `candidate_freeze.json`: candidate source hashes recorded before independent case selection; also binds the unchanged physical model and borrowed frozen components.
- `development_history.json`: five nominal development trials with configurations, hashes, actual per-step metrics and normalized/per-phase chatter. Version 4 worsened chatter/tilt; version 5 was selected. These are not heldout results. Large raw traces and versioned sources are retained separately in the task workspace.
- `results.json`: frozen nominal plus explicit strong-push, tilt and sensor failures, with source hashes and resource measurements.
- `old_lane_comparison.json`: full report/control-trace replay comparisons of old diagnostic, posture and two-step lanes against their unchanged base.

- `registered_cases.json`, `independent_results.json`, `independent_process_records.json`, `actual_perturbation_phases.json` and `INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md`: independently selected cases, separate trace-derived measurements, resource records and limitations. The exact reviewer harness/analysis are retained as `.py.txt` evidence; their absolute paths describe the original review workspace.
- `process_failures.json`: one nominal trace process returned signal 9 during the initial 49-test focused suite; cause and resource reliability remain unresolved. Includes commands, timestamps, known concurrency and source-path diagnosis.
- `validation.json`: the separate full 692-test run passed, all 24 old-lane reports/traces matched and the frozen four-case evaluator reproduced expected outcomes. These successes do not erase the earlier failure.
- `reviewed_source_sha256.json`: final source, script, test and document hashes. `sustained_probe_accepted` is a narrow six-step fixture decision; general walking, balance, standing and hardware flags remain false. No file contains a learned policy or imported historical corpus.

## Resource-verdict correction

Historical files above remain records of the original candidate, not retroactively qualified resource-budget passes. In particular, the archived installed nominal report at 15.80148 cumulative CPU seconds physically completed but exceeded the observed budget; its original acceptance was incorrect. `resource_correction.json` preserves its source hashes and before/after classification. `resource_verdict_freeze.json` binds the separate correction without replacing the historical freeze. New deterministic contract tests do not constitute another nominal physics/performance run, and resource reliability remains unresolved.

`RESOURCE_VERDICT_REVIEW.md`, `resource_review_hashes.json` and `resource_correction_validation.json` record the new independent review and final 17 nonphysics contract checks. `resource_evaluator_regression.json` verifies the archived overage makes the corrected evaluator fail with a resource reason while retaining physical completion. The intermediate malformed-limit launcher issue in review evidence was corrected and re-reviewed; controller and performance tests stayed unchanged.
