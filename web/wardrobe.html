Review what is already in your wardrobe

Declare your actual clothing inventory and minimum ready-to-use needs. Review a saved inventory to build a conditional reuse, repair and surplus checklist before considering purchases. Nothing is bought, donated, discarded or physically inspected here. This screen never calls a model or judges material or religious claims.

Enter your assigned token only for a trusted HTTPS or local loopback API. Tokens and drafts are not stored in browser storage or copied between pages. Scopes separate context, not same-owner permissions. Saved inventories may be recalled by ordinary chat and sent to its configured provider; this dedicated screen stays local to your API.

Ready. Nothing is loaded or saved automatically.

Pages scan 20 owned memories at a time and show typed inventories only, including expired ones. Keep paging if other memories fill a page. Expired inventories cannot build new checklists.

    Saved inventory under review

    No saved inventory is selected.

    Unsaved edits cannot be used. Save changes or review the record again. Changed, expired, deleted or wrong-scope records are rejected by the API. A returned checklist is a snapshot, not automatically retracted by later changes.

    New inventory: choose every field

    Use one row for a group of clothing with the same declared condition, fit, use, ownership and keep decision. Labels must be distinct. Add 1–30 rows, at most 300 total units. For footwear or sets, use one consistent unit, such as a pair, in both quantities and minimums. Do not enter secrets or other people’s private details.

    Your minimum ready-to-use needs

    Explicitly enter 0–50 for every category. These are your chosen needs, not a recommendation, assessment or purchase instruction.

    Ready and fitting items count toward your declared needs only when you explicitly say they are yours. Shared, other-owned and uncertain items need review. A keep decision protects an item from surplus suggestions; this screen never authorizes disposal.

    Leave expiry blank to keep the inventory eligible indefinitely. Editing an existing inventory preserves its expiry; use the memory manager to change expiry or delete it.
