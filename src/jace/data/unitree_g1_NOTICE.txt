The source describes derivation from Unitree's G1 robot model and the addition of primitive collision shapes/contact pairs.

JACE modifications remove 35 zero-density visual mesh geoms and 35 mesh assets plus the unused visual default and mesh directory attribute. The scene include filename is changed. Explicit body inertias, articulated joints, collision shapes/pairs, actuators, solver settings and source keyframes are retained. XML formatting is regenerated. Original source files, hashes and reproducible derivation are included in the source checkout.

This is a modified diagnostic model, not a validated robot/controller, standing or locomotion implementation, or hardware safety claim. No pretrained policy is included or loaded. Names identify source attribution, not endorsement.
